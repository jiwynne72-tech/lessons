1. The Clockmaker’s Secret

In a small village, there lived a clockmaker called Elias. He used to open his workshop at sunrise and would spend the first hour listening to the clocks. These days, however, he is used to working late because customers often arrive after work.

One morning, a traveller brought a stopped clock. “I’ve tried winding it, but nothing happens.” Elias replied, “I remember seeing one like this when I was an apprentice. My teacher told me to be patient and never force a mechanism. I stopped repairing clocks in a hurry a long time ago.”

He tried using a thinner tool, then tried taking the back panel off, and eventually found a tiny piece of dust between two gears. “Never regret asking for help,” Elias said. “And remember to take care of the things you value.”

2. Grammar Notes: Habits & Verb Patterns

used to + infinitive: past habit or state no longer true.

would + infinitive: repeated past actions in a narrative (not states).

be / get used to + noun / -ing: being or becoming accustomed to something.

stop / remember / try / regret + -ing vs. to-infinitive: pattern can change meaning (e.g., stop doing = finish an activity; stop to do = pause in order to do another).

3. Reading Comprehension

1. What did Elias use to do at sunrise?

2. What did Elias's teacher teach him?

3. What caused the clock to stop?
