Grammar Into Focus: Habits, Verb Patterns & Transformations

Review rules and complete the exercises across habits, verb patterns, meaning changes (stop/remember/try), a fable gap-fill, sentence transformations, and video prompts.

1. Present and Past Habits

Used to + infinitive: past states or regular habits no longer true. Would + infinitive: repeated past actions (not states). Continuous + always: persistent/annoying habits.

1. When sharing a room in residence, a roommate ___ things without asking.

2. As a child, I ___ that my dad was a spy.

3. It's so annoying! Someone ___ the notebook.

4. Martha ___ plenty of questions after every lesson.

2. Verb Patterns & Change in Meaning

stop/remember/try + -ing vs. + to-infinitive.

1. Liz stopped ___ coffee (no longer drinks it).

2. Liz stopped ___ some coffee (paused in order to have some).

3. Remember ___ the door before leaving (duty/reminder).
