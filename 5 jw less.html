4. Reading & Video: Masaccio and Renaissance Perspective

Tommaso di Ser Giovanni di Mòne (1401–1428), known as Masaccio, helped shape early Renaissance painting through linear perspective, natural lighting, and realistic emotion.

In the Brancacci Chapel in Florence, frescoes depicting Saint Peter—including The Tribute Money, Saint Peter Healing the Sick with His Shadow, and The Distribution of Alms and Death of Ananias—influenced later artists such as Michelangelo and Raphael.
