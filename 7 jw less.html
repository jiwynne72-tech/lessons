Session Overview

Lesson Notes & Language Review

Summary of grammar structures, natural phrasing, vocabulary, and idioms covered on 12/11/2026.

1. Core Grammar & Syntax

Word Order (S.V.O.M.P.T.): Subject – Verb – Object – Manner – Place – Time. Remember to keep the verb and object together and place time expressions at the end (or beginning) of the sentence.

Grammar Focus Areas: 3rd person singular vs. plural verb agreement; Present Continuous vs. Present Perfect; and revising Have vs. Have got.

2. Word Formation & Suffixes (English vs. Italian)

Noun vs. Adjective: Health (noun) → Healthy (adjective).

Suffix Patterns: -ty (e.g., reality / realtà); -ly (adverbs, equivalent to Italian -mente); and -tion = -zione (e.g., specialization / specializzazione).

3. Key Vocabulary, False Friends & Idioms

Verbs & False Friends: Sprecare = to waste (time/money); Superare = to pass (an exam/test); to control (have power over/manage) vs. controllare (often to check).

Topic & Technical Terms: Developer degree, reality problems, perfectionist, blood clot, metal mesh, and to my satisfaction.

Idiom: A double-edged sword (not “two sides of the sword”) — something that has both advantages and disadvantages.

Language Refinements

Natural Phrasing & “Itanglish” Corrections

Direct translations from Italian replaced with natural, idiomatic English expressions.

Verbs, Prepositions & Collocations

Time Expressions

Original: I do a lot of time...

Issue: Literal translation of Italian fare/mettere tempo. English uses spend or need with time.

Correction: I spend a lot of time... / I need a lot of time...

Dependent Prepositions

Original: Pay attention about...

Issue: Incorrect preposition following attention.

Correction: Pay attention to...

Describing Academic Courses & Syllabi

Original: You study [topics] in this degree...

Issue: Using informal you when describing what a course includes.

Correction: The subject / degree covers... (Also note: I want to do / take a specialization or learn specialized skills).

Discourse Markers & Expressing Opinions

Stating Your Point of View

Original: In my mind...

Issue: Direct transfer from Italian nella mia mente; unnatural for giving opinions.

Correction: In my opinion... / From my point of view... / For me...

Contrasting Two Sides of an Argument

Original: For a part... / On the opposite...

Issue: Calques of da una parte and all’opposto.

Correction: On the one hand... / On the other hand... (Also use The first reason... to structure points).

Generalizing

Original: For all the cases...

Issue: Literal translation of per tutti i casi.

Correction: In every case... / In all cases...
